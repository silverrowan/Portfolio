About Me

I started out as an artist and entrepreneur, then found my love for coding and software development.
There's a clear pattern in my work, whether at an international makeup college, a designer baby blanket company, or an independent physiotherapy clinic. In every job I'm drawn to solving problems, especially where the human/technology interface or logistics fall short. The best solutions aren’t just technical; they’re creative, human, and made for real people.
In the past five years, I tested these instincts by running several small indie businesses that mixed fun, affordability, and fresh ideas. During COVID, what started as making masks for a few friends rapidly snowballed into a niche, but profitable undertaking which helped me save for my first house. As the need for masks was dwindling, I started a tea brand, focusing on quality and affordability, which surprisingly is what lead to realizing that I very much enjoy programming (available tools didn't do what I wanted, so I started hacking together my own). Each project taught me more about turning ideas into reality.
I love coming up with new ideas and taking on unique challenges, whether in coding, the arts, or just for fun. In my free time, I enjoy a cup of tea with my cat Marigold, go on adventures in D&D or indie RPGs, and take walks with my partner along the beaches of Vancouver Island. Getting out into nature and gardening isn’t just a hobby for me, it’s a necessity of modern living.
